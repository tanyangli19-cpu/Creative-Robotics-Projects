# Creative Robotics Portfolio | 创意机器人项目作品集

This repository documents selected robotics projects developed during my study in Creative Robotics. These projects explore the relationship between robotics, interaction, cultural narratives, and performance, combining physical prototyping, sensing, mechanical design, and human–robot interaction.

记录了我在 Creative Robotics 学习期间完成的部分机器人项目。这些项目探索机器人、交互、文化叙事与表演之间的关系，并结合实体原型、传感器、机械设计与人机交互进行实践。

## Project 01 — Xiao Liu Ren Fortune-Telling Robot  
## 项目一：小六壬传统文化机器人

### Overview | 项目简介

Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r9fNzEMsPME

This project explores how the traditional Chinese divination system **Xiao Liu Ren (小六壬)** can be transformed into an interactive robotic experience.

The traditional rules of Xiao Liu Ren are translated into a robotic interaction based on the concept of **Heaven, Earth, and Human (天时、地利、人和)**. Three sensors provide inputs to the system, which are mapped to the corresponding Xiao Liu Ren rules.

The result is expressed through a **robotic flower**. Different outcomes are represented by changes in the **degree of blooming and the colour of the flower**, together with a corresponding textual interpretation.

Rather than attempting to provide definitive answers, the robot is designed as an interactive cultural experience that can offer a small moment of reflection and psychological comfort for people facing difficult decisions.

本项目探索如何将中国传统文化中的 **小六壬规则**转化为一种机器人交互体验。

项目以 **“天时、地利、人和”** 的理念作为交互框架，通过三个传感器获取输入，并将这些信息映射到小六壬对应的规则之中。

最终结果通过一个 **花朵机器人**进行表达。机器人会通过不同的**开花程度与花朵颜色**呈现不同结果，同时提供相应的解文。

这个机器人并不是为了替使用者做出决定，而是希望将传统文化转化为一种具有趣味性和仪式感的交互体验，在展览环境中吸引观众参与，同时也为正在面对选择的人提供一点思考空间与心理慰藉。

### Application | 实际应用

The project was later adapted for the robotic theatre production **One Thousand and One Nights (一千零一夜)**.

Within the performance, the flower robot was transformed from an exhibition interaction into a theatrical character, taking on the role of a **prophet / oracle** who provides predictions as part of the story.

该项目后来被应用于机器人戏剧 **《一千零一夜》**。

在戏剧中，花朵机器人从原本的展览交互装置进一步转化为一个具有叙事功能的机器人角色，并以 **“预言家”** 的身份参与剧情，通过预测和解文成为戏剧叙事的一部分。

## Project 02 — Mecanum-Wheeled Snake Robot  
## 项目二：麦轮车蛇形机器人

### Overview | 项目简介

This project explores a modular approach to creating a snake-like robotic character by combining different robotic structures.

The robot consists of three main parts:

- a **Mecanum-wheel mobile base** for omnidirectional movement;
- a **robotic arm** acting as the snake's head and upper body;
- a **servo-driven articulated structure** forming the snake's tail.

By combining mobility, articulated movement, and a character-oriented physical form, the project transforms existing robotic components into a robotic performer designed specifically for a theatrical environment.

本项目探索如何通过不同机器人结构的组合，构建一个具有蛇形特征的机器人角色。

机器人主要由三个部分组成：

- 使用 **麦克纳姆轮移动底盘**实现灵活的全向移动；
- 使用 **机械臂**作为蛇的头部与上半身；
- 使用 **舵机与连接结构**组成可活动的蛇尾。

项目通过移动能力、关节运动与角色化外形的结合，将不同机器人模块重新组合成一个适用于戏剧表演场景的机器人角色。

### Application | 实际应用

The snake robot was developed for the robotic theatre production **One Thousand and One Nights (一千零一夜)** and appeared as part of the **Snake Queen** scene.

In this context, the robot was not only a technical prototype but also part of the stage narrative and visual performance. Its movement and physical form were designed to support the atmosphere and character of the theatrical scene.

该蛇形机器人同样参与了机器人戏剧 **《一千零一夜》**，并作为 **“蛇女王”场景**的一部分出现在舞台中。

在这一应用中，机器人不仅是一个技术原型，同时也是舞台叙事和视觉表现的一部分。机器人的移动方式、身体结构和蛇形形态共同服务于戏剧场景和角色氛围的塑造。
